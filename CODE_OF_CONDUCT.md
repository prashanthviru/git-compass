# Code of Conduct

Git Compass should be a friendly place to ask questions, report problems and contribute, whatever your experience, background or identity.

## What we expect
- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and remember that everyone here gives their time freely.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person. Explain your reasoning and stay open to other views.
- Welcome newcomers and help them find their way.
- Respect people's time: search existing issues first, and give enough detail for others to help.

## What is not acceptable
- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and any unwelcome attention.

## Scope and enforcement
This applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the GitHub profile of [@prashanthviru](https://github.com/prashanthviru). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove content, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban anyone who breaks this code.
